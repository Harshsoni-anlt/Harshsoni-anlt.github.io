title: Can a 3B decision model run the inbox?
model: Liquid d1-3B (local, Apple M1 Pro)
kind: tested
result: A 3B model on a laptop, passing only its unsure calls to the cloud, matched cloud-only accuracy on 300 banking messages while keeping 68% of calls local (96.1% accurate on those). One dataset; re-test on messier data pending.
tag: Tested · repo pending
---

**Setup.** 300 real bank-support messages (Banking77): 100 fraud, 100 lookalikes that read like fraud but aren't, 100 others. Two decisions each: a fraud flag and one of 7 teams. The local model returns a probability per answer, no generated text. The cloud baseline is Gemini 3.5 Flash-Lite, asked to state its confidence.

| | d1-3B, local | Flash-Lite, cloud |
|---|---|---|
| Fraud accuracy | 0.927 | 0.967 |
| Fraud AUROC | 0.971 | 0.985 |
| Fraud calibration error (ECE) | 0.058 | 0.032 |
| Routing accuracy | 0.873 | 0.883 |
| Latency | 633 ms p50 per message | 4.4 s per 25-message call |

**The gate.** At a 0.8 confidence threshold the local model keeps 68% of messages, 96.1% accurate on those, and escalates the rest. Combined accuracy 0.897 vs 0.877 cloud-only. The gap is 6 messages out of 300, so the honest claim is *matches the cloud with a third of the cloud calls*, not "beats it".

**Also measured.** Gemini 3.8 Flash on the free tier took 203.8 s for one 5-message call; Flash-Lite took 2.5 s.
